The Leading Provider of Ventilation Systems

Established since 1998, Dongil Co., Ltd. is a leading provider of ventilation systems committed to "creating wind that is beneficial to life." As a company that values customer satisfaction and social responsibility, we specialize in designing, purchasing, manufacturing, and selling high-quality fans and blowers.

Our expansion is fuelled by a strategic partnership with South Korean companies Dongil Machinery, which enables us to lead the market with our own original brand. Through continuous research and development, we strive to deliver the highest quality products and thorough after-sales service to meet the evolving needs of our global customers.
