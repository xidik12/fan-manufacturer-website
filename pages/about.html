Company Profile

30+ Years of Ventilation Excellence

Established in 1998, Dongil Co., Ltd. is a leading provider of ventilation systems in Korea and Cambodia, committed to "creating wind that is beneficial to life." With an unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction, quality, and social responsibility, we have earned the trust of engineers, contractors, and building operators across Southeast Asia and beyond.

Our comprehensive product portfolio spans sirocco fans, air foil fans, axial fans, duct in-line fans, roof fans, wall ventilation, and large ceiling fans — each designed and manufactured to meet the most demanding standards. From compact residential ventilation to large-scale industrial and commercial systems, Dongil delivers the right solution for every environment.

Our expansion is fuelled by a strategic partnership with South Korean companies Dongil Machinery and Fanzic, enabling us to lead the market by developing our own original brand. We thoroughly manage all processes from design, purchase, production, to sales and after-sales service.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

From our founding in South Korea to a growing regional presence, our journey has been defined by quality, innovation, and a dedication to beneficial ventilation.

1998

Company Founded

Dongil Co., Ltd. was established in South Korea with a vision to manufacture world-class ventilation products.

2005

Product Line Expansion

Expanded product range to include sirocco fans, air foil fans, axial fans, and in-line duct fans.

2010

International Expansion

Began exporting to Southeast Asian markets including Cambodia, Vietnam, and Thailand.

2015

Cambodia Operations

Established Dongil International office in Phnom Penh to serve the growing Cambodian construction market.
