Company Profile

Over 30 Years of Ventilation Excellence

Founded in 1994, AeroFlow has grown from a small engineering workshop into one of the world's leading manufacturers of ventilation solutions. With an unwavering commitment to innovation, quality, and energy efficiency, we have earned the trust of engineers, consultants, and building operators across more than 80 countries.

Our comprehensive product portfolio spans axial, centrifugal, in-line, jet, and roof exhaust fans, each designed and manufactured to meet the most demanding international standards. From compact domestic ventilation to large-scale tunnel and industrial systems, AeroFlow delivers the right solution for every environment.

At the heart of our operations lies a passion for engineering excellence. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, rigorous quality control processes, and dedicated R&D centers ensure that every AeroFlow product delivers superior performance, reliability, and energy savings throughout its operational life.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

From a single workshop to a global leader in ventilation technology, our journey has been defined by ambition, innovation, and excellence.

1994

Company Founded

AeroFlow was established with a vision to manufacture world-class ventilation products. Our first facility began operations with 25 employees and a single product line of axial fans.

2000

First International Export

Expanding beyond domestic borders, AeroFlow delivered its first international shipment, marking the beginning of a global export operation that would eventually reach over 80 countries.

2005

ISO 9001 Certification

Achieved ISO 9001 Quality Management certification, underscoring our dedication to consistent quality across all manufacturing processes and product lines.

2012

Regional Expansion

Opened new regional offices and distribution centers across the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Europe, bringing AeroFlow products and support closer to customers worldwide.

2018

Innovation Center Opened

Inaugurated a dedicated R&D and Innovation Center featuring advanced CFD simulation labs, acoustic testing chambers, and a prototype workshop to accelerate product development.

2024

10,000th Project Milestone

Celebrated the completion of our 10,000th project, a landmark tunnel ventilation system, solidifying AeroFlow's position as a global leader in the ventilation industry.

Innovation & Technology

Pushing the Boundaries of Ventilation Engineering

AeroFlow invests over 5% of annual revenue into research and development, driving continuous advancement in fan aerodynamics, motor efficiency, noise reduction, and smart control systems. Our Innovation Center houses some of the most advanced testing and simulation facilities in the industry.

EC Motor Technology

Electronically commutated motors delivering up to 70% energy savings compared to traditional AC motors, with integrated speed control and monitoring.

CFD Analysis & Simulation

Computational Fluid Dynamics modeling to optimize blade profiles, housing geometry, and airflow patterns for maximum efficiency and minimum noise.

Smart Controls & IoT

IoT-enabled fan controllers with real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance alerts, and BMS integration for intelligent building ventilation management.

Advanced Testing Facilities

AMCA-certified airflow testing chambers, acoustic labs, vibration analysis, and endurance testing rigs ensuring every product meets the highest performance benchmarks.
