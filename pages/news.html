New Smart Large Ceiling Fan Series Launched

Dongil introduces its new Smart Large Ceiling Fan series featuring app-controlled variable speeds, energy monitoring, and whisper-quiet operation ideal for tropical climates.

The new Smart Large Ceiling Fan (HVLS — High Volume Low Speed) series delivers powerful, energy-efficient air circulation for warehouses, factories, and large commercial spaces. With blade diameters ranging from 3m to 7.3m, a single unit can cool up to 1,400 m² of floor area.

Key features include app-controlled variable speed settings, real-time energy consumption monitoring, brushless DC motor technology for ultra-quiet operation, and compatibility with BMS building automation systems. The series is engineered specifically for the hot, humid conditions of Southeast Asia.

High-Temperature Industrial Fan Series Released

Dongil releases its latest generation of high-temperature fans capable of handling process gases up to 1000°C, designed for steel plants, glass factories, and industrial kiln applications.

The new high-temperature fan series features ceramic and stainless steel impeller construction, heat-resistant bearings, and thermally isolated motor mounting systems. Units are available in centrifugal and axial configurations rated for continuous operation at up to 1000°C process gas temperatures.

Applications include kiln exhaust handling, steel mill furnace ventilation, glass factory fume extraction, and petrochemical process ventilation. Custom engineering is available for non-standard temperatures, corrosive gases, and spark-resistant requirements.

Energy-Efficient Ventilation in Cambodia's Construction Boom

As Cambodia's construction sector accelerates, the demand for compliant, energy-efficient ventilation systems has never been higher. Our latest insight paper explores key design strategies.

Cambodia's rapid urbanisation is driving unprecedented demand for compliant building services, including mechanical ventilation. This insight paper outlines how M&E engineers can meet energy performance targets by specifying EC motor-driven fans, optimizing duct system layouts, and leveraging demand-controlled ventilation strategies.

Key strategies covered include backward-curved air foil fan selection for HVAC AHUs, variable speed drives for partial-load efficiency, and HVLS ceiling fans as a low-energy alternative to air conditioning in large open spaces — all available from Dongil International's Cambodia stock.

Fire Safety Ventilation Requirements for Southeast Asian Buildings

A comprehensive overview of smoke extraction and stairwell pressurization requirements applicable to commercial and residential developments across the ASEAN region.
