AeroFlow Launches Next-Generation EC Motor Fan Series

AeroFlow is proud to announce the launch of its next-generation EC (Electronically Commutated) motor fan series, representing a major leap forward in ventilation technology. The new lineup delivers up to 70% energy savings compared to traditional AC motor fans, while offering superior speed control, reduced noise levels, and extended operational lifespan.

Designed for HVAC, commercial, and industrial applications, the EC motor series features integrated smart controls compatible with BMS systems, making it the ideal choice for modern, energy-conscious building projects worldwide.
