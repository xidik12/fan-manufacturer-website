FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive.

You can request a quote by filling out the contact form on this page and selecting "Quote Request" as the subject. Alternatively, you can email us directly at sales@aeroflowfans.com with your project specifications including airflow requirements, static pressure, application type, and quantity. Our engineering team will typically respond with a detailed quotation within 24-48 business hours.

Yes, AeroFlow specializes in custom-engineered ventilation solutions. Our in-house engineering team can design and manufacture fans tailored to your specific requirements, including custom sizes, materials, motor configurations, coatings, and certifications. We work closely with consultants and contractors to ensure the perfect fit for every project, no matter how unique the specifications.

Lead times vary depending on the product type and customization level. Standard catalogue products are typically available within 2-4 weeks. Custom-engineered solutions may require 6-10 weeks from order confirmation. For urgent project needs, we offer expedited manufacturing on select product lines. Contact our sales team for specific lead time estimates based on your requirements.

AeroFlow provides comprehensive installation support including detailed installation manuals, CAD drawings, and technical guidance. For larger projects, our field service engineers can conduct on-site supervision, commissioning, and performance testing. We also offer training programs for maintenance personnel to ensure optimal long-term operation of your ventilation systems.

All AeroFlow products come with a standard 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. Extended warranty options of up to 5 years are available for select product ranges. Our warranty includes replacement parts and technical support. For detailed warranty terms and conditions, please contact our after-sales team at support@aeroflowfans.com.
