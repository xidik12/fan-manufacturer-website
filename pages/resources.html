Case Studies

Metro Highway Tunnel

Tunnel Ventilation System

Challenge

A 4.2 km twin-tube highway tunnel required emergency smoke extraction capable of handling 400 MW fire scenarios with strict noise limitations near residential areas.

Solution

Deployed 48 reversible jet fans with acoustic silencers, integrated with SCADA-based fire detection and automated ventilation control.

Result

Exceeded performance targets by 15%, reduced energy consumption by 35% through variable speed drives, and met all acoustic requirements.

Regional Medical Center

Hospital HVAC Upgrade

Challenge

A 500-bed hospital needed to upgrade aging HVAC fans while maintaining 24/7 operations in critical care, operating theaters, and isolation wards with strict air quality requirements.

Solution

Phased replacement of 120 AHU fans with EC motor backward curved units featuring BMS integration for demand-controlled ventilation.

Result

Annual energy savings of $180,000, 60% noise reduction, zero downtime during phased installation, and HEPA-grade air quality maintained.

City Center Mall Parking

Car Park Ventilation System

Challenge

A 6-level underground car park with 2,400 spaces required CO/NO2 monitoring ventilation with smoke extraction capability for fire safety compliance.

Solution

Installed 72 impulse jet fans with CO/NO2 sensors and CFD-optimized placement, replacing traditional ducted ventilation to free up ceiling height.

Result

40% reduction in construction costs, 50% energy savings through demand-based operation, and full compliance with local fire safety regulations.

SteelMax Manufacturing

Industrial Plant Ventilation

Challenge

A 25,000 m2 steel fabrication plant with extreme heat loads, fume extraction requirements, and corrosive atmosphere needed comprehensive ventilation overhaul.

Solution

Custom-engineered high-temperature axial fans with corrosion-resistant coatings, paired with roof extractors and make-up air units for balanced ventilation.

Result

Ambient temperature reduced by 12 degrees C, worker productivity increased 22%, and the system met all occupational health exposure limits.
