Car Park Ventilation

Enclosed and underground car parks present unique ventilation challenges -- from carbon monoxide accumulation during normal operation to rapid smoke extraction during fire emergencies. AeroFlow's jet fan systems use impulse ventilation to efficiently move air without traditional ductwork, reducing construction costs and improving coverage.

Jet fan impulse ventilation eliminates the need for large ductwork, freeing up ceiling height and reducing installation cost
CO and NO2 sensor integration enables demand-controlled ventilation, running fans only when pollutant levels require it
Emergency smoke extraction mode with reversible jet fans rated to 300C for 2 hours per EN 12101-3
CFD-validated designs ensure uniform air distribution across the entire parking area

Smoke Extraction

Fire safety ventilation is not optional -- it is life-saving infrastructure. AeroFlow smoke extraction fans are tested and certified to operate at extreme temperatures during a fire event, maintaining clear escape routes and enabling firefighter access. Our systems comply with EN 12101-3 and local fire code requirements worldwide.

Axial and centrifugal smoke extraction fans rated F400 (400C for 2 hours) per EN 12101-3 certification
Stairwell and lobby pressurization fans that maintain positive pressure to keep escape routes smoke-free
Emergency ventilation fans with automatic fire alarm integration and backup power supply connections
Full compliance with international fire codes including BS 7346, NFPA 92, and EN 12101-3
