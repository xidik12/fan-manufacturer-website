Smoke Extraction

Life Safety Smoke Extraction Systems

Fire safety is paramount. Dongil's smoke extraction fans are F300, F400, and F600 rated, ensuring reliable operation during a fire event for the critical window needed to evacuate occupants safely. Used in car parks, atriums, corridors, and stairwells.

  • Atrium smoke management and mechanical smoke control
